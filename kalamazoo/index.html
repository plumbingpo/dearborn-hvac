Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Kalamazoo

Most mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, central air usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the worst heat wave of the year.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a miserable night.
